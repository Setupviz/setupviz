# Stock monitor stand references

Drawings look down from above, with the user at the bottom. Stand span and cabinet depth are in millimetres. The polygons simplify fillets, rubber pads, and small openings. The fit check uses the outside of each modeled foot or plate, including its interior for solid plates. Only the stand occupies desk area. The panel is drawn above it.

Panel positions are approximate at neutral tilt. Side and overhead images locate the front face relative to the stand. Oblique photos establish the stand family but do not uniquely determine the front offset. Those rows use an estimate. Models sharing stock hardware share an outline.
