Privacy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Who we are

SetupViz is run by Freddy Rosa ("SetupViz", "we", "us"). This page explains what information the site handles. SetupViz has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name or email.

On this device

SetupViz saves your desk size, the unit you pick, and your grip, hand, and use answers in this browser. It also saves the setup the builder last showed. That stays on this device. SetupViz does not set a cookie.

To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser settings (often under "Cookies and site data" or "Website data"). Private or incognito windows delete it when you close them.

Amazon cookies

After you follow a link, Amazon may set Amazon cookies on Amazon's own site. Those cookies belong to Amazon.

Share links

When you share a setup, the link itself contains your desk size, the products you picked and where you placed them, and your answers about grip, hand size and how you use your desk. Anyone who has the link can open it and see that setup. It doesn't include your name, email or anything else about you. Only share it with people you're happy to show your desk to.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the site is used. It counts page views, the site you came from, your country, your browser and device type, and how fast pages load. It sets no cookies and doesn't use fingerprinting. Results are only shown as totals, so we never see a profile of any one visitor. Cloudflare processes this data for us.

If you email us

If you email hello@setupviz.com, we get your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply, and delete it when it's no longer needed.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your personal information, show third-party ads, or build profiles of visitors.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because SetupViz keeps almost nothing about you, there is usually nothing on our side to send or delete. To make a request, email hello@setupviz.com. We'll answer within 30 days.

Children

SetupViz is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Where data is processed

SetupViz runs on Cloudflare, which may process requests in the United States and other countries.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.

Contact

Questions about this page go to hello@setupviz.com.
