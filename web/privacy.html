Privacy

Last updated October 17, 2026

On this device

SetupViz saves your desk size, the unit you pick, and your grip, hand, and use answers in this browser. It also saves the setup the builder last showed. That stays on this device. SetupViz does not set a cookie.

Amazon cookies

After you follow a link, Amazon may set Amazon cookies on Amazon's own site. Those cookies belong to Amazon.

Share links

When you share a setup, the link itself contains your desk size, the products you picked and where you placed them, and your answers about grip, hand size and how you use your desk. Anyone who has the link can open it and see that setup. It doesn't include your name, email or anything else about you. Only share it with people you're happy to show your desk to.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the site is used. It counts page views, the site you came from, your country, your browser and device type, and how fast pages load. It sets no cookies and doesn't use fingerprinting. Results are only shown as totals, so we never see a profile of any one visitor. Cloudflare processes this data for us.
